Navassa, NC 28451 is an incorporated town in Brunswick County, chartered in 1977, with 1,367 residents at the 2020 census. It sits roughly 5 miles west of downtown Wilmington on the west bank of the river, reached across the Cape Fear River and by the I-140 interchange. Mail here uses the 28451 ZIP code that Leland shares.

The town grew around a 19th-century fertilizer industry. The Navassa Guano Factory opened in 1869, and the railroad bridges built in 1867 tied the town to Wilmington. Today the older streets near that history sit alongside newer subdivisions as Navassa has become one of the faster-growing towns in the county.

Navassa Township District Park and a close-knit town

Navassa Township District Park hosts community events, including the annual Homecoming Festival. The town has strong ties to Gullah Geechee heritage, and many families have lived here for generations. Long-tenured owners in older homes tend to want honest repairs rather than quick replacements.

That fits how we work. If a repair will hold, we say so. If a pipe has reached the end of its life, we show you why and give you a clear price first.

Low ground between two rivers

Navassa sits at about 23 feet above sea level at the junction of the Brunswick and Northeast Cape Fear rivers, among tidal freshwater marshes. The ground stays wet, and heavy rain finds it hard to drain away. Foundations, crawl spaces, and buried lines all feel that moisture.

Persistent wet soil also affects buried sewer lines. Roots, shifting ground, and standing water can crack or offset a pipe over time. Our Sewer Line Repair service starts with a camera inspection so we repair only what the picture shows.
