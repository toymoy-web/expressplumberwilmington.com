Where Wilmington Express Plumbing works

We cover Wilmington and the surrounding New Hanover County area, plus Navassa and Leland across the river in Brunswick County. See the dedicated page for your specific town below, or call to confirm coverage for your exact address.

Wilmington ZIP codes we cover

Wilmington spans several distinct ZIP codes, and each one has a somewhat different housing-age and foundation profile. ZIP 28401 covers the historic downtown core along the Cape Fear River, where older homes are more likely to have cast iron or clay sewer laterals. ZIP 28403 sits near UNCW, with a mix of rental housing and mid-century construction.

ZIP 28405 is near the airport, and ZIP 28411 covers the Porters Neck area in the north near Smith Creek Park and Laney High School, mostly newer slab-foundation construction. ZIP 28409 and ZIP 28412 together cover a large share of the city's southern residential growth, including the Myrtle Grove area, and tend toward newer slab and crawl-space homes built from the 1990s onward.

Why the ZIP code you're in actually matters

A plumbing call in ZIP 28401 usually starts from a different assumption than one in ZIP 28411, and it's not just about distance from downtown. A pre-1960 home near the Cape Fear River is more likely to still have its original cast iron sewer lateral, a material that develops rough interior joints over decades and catches roots and grease more easily than modern PVC.

A 2005-built home in Porters Neck or Myrtle Grove almost certainly sits on a slab foundation with PEX or copper supply lines. The more common call there is a slab leak or a water heater nearing the end of its service life, not a root-clogged sewer line. Knowing which pattern applies to your address before we arrive means we bring the right diagnostic tool on the first visit instead of the second.

Water and sewer service across the county

The Cape Fear Public Utility Authority manages water and sewer service for the City of Wilmington and New Hanover County, and it also extends sewer service to Carolina Beach, Kure Beach, and Wrightsville Beach under an inter-jurisdictional agreement. Knowing which utility serves a given address matters for anything involving a water meter reading, a service line dispute, or a backflow testing requirement.

Plumbing contractors working anywhere in this coverage area are licensed through the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors, regardless of which town the job is in.

Beyond Wilmington's city limits

We also field calls from New Hanover County addresses outside Wilmington's city limits. Inside the greater Wilmington area those include Ogden, Porters Neck, and Myrtle Grove. On the coast we cover Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, and Kure Beach, plus Castle Hayne to the north. Each of those pages covers the real, distinct plumbing profile of that specific town rather than a generic beach-town description.

Across the Cape Fear River in Brunswick County, we cover Navassa and Leland, and to the north in Pender County we cover Hampstead. Brunswick County addresses run on a different water utility and a different construction era than the coastal New Hanover County towns, so we confirm the provider for each address.

We can also reach Southport, Oak Island, and other southern Brunswick County addresses for scheduled work, but those are long drives from our Wilmington base, so call first to confirm timing.

Hurricane season and coastal coverage

Coastal New Hanover County addresses face a real, seasonal consideration that inland customers don't: storm surge and heavy rainfall can push water back through an untested backflow preventer. The City of Wilmington's Building Department recommends checking this before hurricane season each year. It's a quick appointment we're glad to schedule anywhere in our coverage area. Call (919) 944-7281 to book one.

Homes closer to the Cape Fear River and the coastal beach towns tend to see crawl-space flooding first during heavy rain events. That's why we recommend checking sump pump function and crawl-space vents as part of the same seasonal visit, rather than waiting for a flooded crawl space to force the issue.

Response times by area

Calls from Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, or Kure Beach add some drive time, especially during summer beach traffic, so an emergency call from those areas is still a priority, but the honest window may run a bit longer than a downtown Wilmington call.
