A family-centric community built around golf course neighborhoods

Hampstead has a notably higher share of households with children than the surrounding county average. Much of that growth has landed in planned golf-course communities like Castle Bay and Olde Point, along with newer developments built to serve young families moving up from Wilmington.

That family-first growth pattern means a lot of Hampstead plumbing calls involve homes still within their first decade or two. That's a profile closer to Leland's new-construction pattern than to the older homes we service near downtown Wilmington.

The area sits close to Topsail Island, and homes nearer the water share some of the same coastal exposure concerns as our beach-town service areas, even though Hampstead itself is a mainland community.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Hampstead and the neighboring 28429 and 28411 ZIP codes, covering Castle Hayne and the Porters Neck side of Wilmington. Slab Leak Detection and Water Heater Installation are the two calls we see most often from that corridor.

Straddling two counties creates real, practical confusion

Some Hampstead-area addresses technically fall in New Hanover County near the Wilmington line, while the community's identity, schools, and much of its growth sit firmly in Pender County. When you call, telling us your specific ZIP or nearest cross street helps us confirm coverage and travel time accurately.

This growth has outpaced some infrastructure in the area, and newer subdivisions sometimes have plumbing connections completed closer to move-in than an older, established neighborhood would. Checking those connections during the first year of ownership is a reasonable, proactive step.

What rapid, family-driven growth means for scheduling

A community full of young families tends to run on tighter schedules, school drop-offs, after-school activities, than a retiree-heavy market, and we try to work around that rather than assume everyone has flexible daytime availability.

Because Hampstead has grown so quickly, we've also learned to double-check new addresses against current maps rather than rely solely on GPS for a very recently built home. If your address is in a new phase of a larger subdivision, mentioning that when you call helps us find you faster. Reach us at (919) 944-7281 any time.
