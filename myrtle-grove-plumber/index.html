Myrtle Grove, NC 28409 is a census-designated place in New Hanover County with 11,476 residents at the 2020 census, up from 8,875 in 2010. Locals still call the commercial hub at College Road (NC 132) and Carolina Beach Road (US 421) Monkey Junction. The name comes from a gas station that kept live monkeys to draw customers from the late 1930s into the mid 1970s.

The neighborhoods around the junction include Masonboro Forest, Whiskey Branch, Georgetowne, Bexley, Hickory Knoll, Sentry Oaks, Belle Meade Plantation, and Saylor's Watch. Several were built in the late 1990s and early 2000s, so many homes are now at the age where first-generation fixtures and water heaters start to fail.

We dispatch to Myrtle Grove from our Wilmington base, and non-emergency estimates are free. Call (919) 944-7281 to schedule, or use the emergency line at any hour.

Homes from the late 1990s are reaching their repair years

A water heater from the first wave of building here is usually on its second or third unit by now. Original toilet fill valves, faucet cartridges, and supply stops are often on their second round too. None of this is dramatic, but it adds up to steady calls for repair and replacement.

When the same fixture fails twice, we look at the cause instead of swapping parts again. Water pressure, mineral buildup, and a worn shutoff can each be behind a repeat failure. Our Water Heater Repair page covers what we check before recommending a replacement.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Myrtle Grove and the neighboring 28403, 28428, and 28480 ZIP codes, from the Carolina Beach Road corridor out toward Masonboro and down to Carolina Beach. Two ZIP codes, one local market

ZIP 28409 covers the eastern side of Myrtle Grove toward Masonboro Sound, and it includes Myrtle Grove Middle School and Masonboro Elementary. ZIP 28412 covers the western and central side around Pine Valley Elementary and Eugene Ashley High School, along with Cape Fear Academy. Residents often use both without thinking about it.

We treat the two as one market because the plumbing conditions are shared. Both sit on low, flat ground that drains slowly after heavy rain.

Monkey Junction retail, restaurants, and small businesses

The intersection of College Road and Carolina Beach Road is one of the densest retail clusters in the county. Restaurants and shops here produce heavy grease and fixture loads that a home never sees. Our Commercial Plumbing work covers drains, water heaters, and fixture repair for these businesses.

A restaurant that loses its drain on a Friday night cannot wait until Monday. That is why the emergency line matters just as much on the commercial side as it does for a flooded bathroom.

Low ground near Masonboro Sound and the Intracoastal

Neighborhoods such as Hickory Knoll sit close to the Intracoastal Waterway, where the ground stays wet and the water table is shallow. Crawl spaces in these areas collect moisture after storms, and a sump pump that fails at the wrong time lets it pool. Our Sump Pump Repair service covers testing, repair, and replacement.

Tropical systems are the real stress test. Before the season starts, a quick check of drains, sump pumps, and backflow devices costs far less than a flooded crawl space.
