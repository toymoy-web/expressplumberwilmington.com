Water Filtration Installation in Wilmington, NC

Taste, odor, sediment, or just wanting cleaner water at the tap. We install whole-home and point-of-use filtration systems sized to what your specific water actually needs.

Call (919) 944-7281, 24/7

Water filtration installation in Wilmington, NC covers whole-home systems that treat water as it enters the house, and point-of-use systems like an under-sink or refrigerator filter that treat water at a single fixture. This is different from a water softener, which addresses mineral hardness specifically rather than taste, odor, or sediment.

Most requests come from one of two directions: a specific concern, chlorine taste or odor, sediment, or a well-water quality question, or a general preference for filtered water without a specific problem driving it. Both are valid reasons to install a system, and the right system depends on which one applies.

Why this happens in Wilmington

What actually varies across Wilmington and New Hanover County water

CFPUA municipal water, treated for the City of Wilmington and New Hanover County, uses chlorine or chloramine disinfection. Some customers notice that as a taste or smell at the tap, even though the water is safe. A basic carbon filtration stage addresses that directly.

Well water, more common in unincorporated parts of New Hanover County, varies address by address and can carry sediment, iron, or other characteristics a municipal customer never encounters. That's why we test well water specifically before recommending a filtration setup, rather than install the same system regardless of source.

Signs to watch for

Signs filtration is worth considering

  • A noticeable chlorine taste or smell from tap water.
  • Visible sediment or cloudiness, especially on well water.
  • A preference for filtered water for drinking and cooking without a specific complaint.
  • Well water that's never been tested for quality.
  • Wanting to protect appliances and fixtures from sediment buildup.
Neighborhoods and landmarks

Filtration for taste, sediment, and well water

Families in Leland and Navassa often ask about taste and chlorine in municipal water. Homes on private wells in Hampstead and Castle Hayne more often need sediment or iron filtration.

We start with a water test, then recommend the simplest system that solves the actual problem.

If you're not sure what kind of system you actually need

Most people asking about this don't know the difference between a whole-home system, an under-sink system, and a softener. That's completely normal, the terminology isn't intuitive. The real question is what's actually bothering you about the water, taste at the faucet, sediment throughout the house, or general water quality, and we figure out the right system type from there.

A whole-home system treats everything entering the house, useful for sediment or chlorine taste throughout every tap and appliance. A point-of-use system treats water at one location, often enough for someone who only cares about drinking and cooking water.

How we find and fix it

How we determine the right system

For municipal water, the conversation is usually about taste and odor, and a whole-home carbon filtration stage is a common, straightforward solution.

For well water, we test first, checking for sediment, iron, pH, and other relevant factors, since the right filtration setup depends entirely on what the water actually contains rather than a generic assumption.

Installation includes sizing the system to household water usage and setting up a realistic filter change schedule, since a filtration system only works as well as its maintenance.
