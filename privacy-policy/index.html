Privacy Policy

Wilmington Express Plumbing ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page describes, in plain terms, what information we collect when you contact us and how it's used.

When you call or email us, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address, and a description of the plumbing issue, solely to schedule and complete the requested service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with our own staff and subcontractors only as needed to complete a job.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@expressplumberwilmington.com.
