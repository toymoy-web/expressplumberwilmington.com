Thirteen miles of coastline means real variation, not one uniform island

The former Long Beach section, now central and western Oak Island, and the former Yaupon Beach section further east each carry some of their pre-merger character. That's true in housing age and style, even 25 years after the towns combined.

A property near the Oak Island Lighthouse and the eastern end, closer to Caswell Beach, sits in an older, more established part of the island. A home further west toward the Intracoastal side may reflect a different, sometimes newer, construction era.

With well over 100 public beach and waterway access points, more than any other coastal town in the state, exterior fixtures and outdoor plumbing here see real, constant public-facing use in a way a private residential street doesn't.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Oak Island and the neighboring 28461 ZIP code across the causeway in Southport. Repiping and Water Heater Installation are the two calls that come up most often across that part of Brunswick County.

A south-facing island brings a specific kind of exposure

Oak Island is one of the only North Carolina barrier islands that faces directly south, which means properties here get sun and salt exposure across a longer daily arc than an east-facing beach town experiences. That's a real, if subtle, difference in how quickly exposed fittings and outdoor fixtures weather.

The island's age and established-ownership pattern also means many homes have had decades to accumulate real plumbing history, sometimes well-maintained, sometimes original. That's similar to what we see at Kure Beach, but across a much larger and more varied stretch of coastline.

A large island means real drive-time variation across the coverage area

Nearly 13 miles from end to end, Oak Island is genuinely larger than any single service area we cover on the New Hanover County beach towns. A call near Caswell Beach on the eastern end and a call near the western tip aren't a quick trip apart, so mentioning your specific area when you call helps us give an accurate arrival estimate.

The causeway connecting Oak Island to Southport and the mainland is the only vehicle route on and off the island. That's similar to the single-bridge access at Wrightsville Beach and Carolina Beach, worth knowing if timing matters for your call.
