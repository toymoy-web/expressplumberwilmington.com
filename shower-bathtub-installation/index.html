Shower & Bathtub Installation in Wilmington, NC

A tub-to-shower conversion, a new bathtub, or a full shower replacement. We handle the plumbing side correctly, from the valve behind the wall to the drain underneath.

Call (919) 944-7281, 24/7

Shower and bathtub installation in Wilmington, NC covers the plumbing work behind a bathroom renovation. That means relocating or replacing supply valves, installing new drain lines sized correctly for the fixture, and connecting a new shower or tub to the home's existing plumbing.

This is plumbing work specifically, not the tile, surround, or fixture selection itself. We do coordinate closely with whatever contractor or design plan is driving the renovation, so the plumbing rough-in matches the final fixture correctly the first time.

Why this happens in Wilmington

What matters for installations in older vs. newer Wilmington homes

In older homes near downtown Wilmington, a bathroom renovation often uncovers galvanized supply lines or an undersized drain that was never meant to handle a modern shower's flow rate. Addressing that during the installation, rather than connecting new fixtures to old, undersized plumbing, is the difference between a shower that performs well and one that disappoints from day one.

In newer construction, the plumbing is usually sound, and the work is more straightforward. That could mean relocating a valve for a new layout, or swapping a tub for a walk-in shower where the existing drain location works for the new design.

Signs to watch for

When to bring in a plumber before the renovation starts

  • Converting a bathtub to a walk-in or curbless shower.
  • Relocating a shower valve or showerhead to a new wall position.
  • Installing a larger or higher-flow showerhead than the existing plumbing was designed for.
  • Discovering older galvanized supply lines during a renovation.
  • A full bathroom remodel that includes new fixture placement.
Neighborhoods and landmarks

Bathroom projects from historic homes to beach condos

Remodels in historic homes near Sunset Park and Carolina Heights often uncover old supply lines and drains behind the tub. We update them while the walls are open.

Beach condos on Wrightsville Beach and Carolina Beach have tight access and shared stacks, so we plan around building rules and the neighboring units.

How we find and fix it

How we approach an installation

We confirm the existing supply line and drain can support the new fixture's specifications before installation starts, rather than assume compatibility and find out otherwise mid-project.

For a valve relocation, we run new supply lines to the correct position and pressure-test the connection before the wall is closed up. A leak discovered after tile work is a much more expensive fix than one caught during rough-in.

Drain work follows the same principle: correctly sized and sloped for the specific fixture, checked before any finish work covers it up.
