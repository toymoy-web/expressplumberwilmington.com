How the pages on this list differ from each other

A few pairs on this list sound similar but cover genuinely different jobs. We've kept them separate on purpose, rather than merging them into one page that would leave a reader unsure which part actually applies to them.

Sewer Line Repair fixes a specific damaged section a camera inspection finds. Sewer Line Replacement means the line has failed broadly enough that a full new section makes more sense than repeated spot fixes. Repiping and Water Line Repair & Replacement split the same way, but for supply water rather than sewer: repiping is the plumbing inside the house, the water line is the buried service line from the meter to the house.

Leak Detection covers general hidden leaks. Slab Leak Detection & Repair gets its own page because a leak under a concrete slab genuinely needs different equipment and different repair access than a leak in a wall or crawl space. Not sure which of these fits what you're seeing? Call (919) 944-7281 and describe it.

Emergency vs. scheduled work

Not every call needs a truck dispatched within the hour, and we'll tell you that honestly rather than treat every job as an emergency. A burst pipe, an active leak soaking a wall, or a sewer backup affecting multiple fixtures gets priority dispatch any time, day or night. A slow drain, a running toilet, or a water heater that's simply aging out gets scheduled at a time that actually works for your day.

That distinction matters for pricing too. Emergency dispatch outside normal hours costs more than a scheduled daytime appointment, and we say so upfront on the phone rather than let you find out after the job is done.
